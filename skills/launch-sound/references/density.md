# Sound density

Measured with `skills/launch-review/scripts/review.py` (librosa onsets per second in 4 s windows).

| Film | Onsets per second | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| A strong SaaS launch film (reference, 2026-09) | 2.5 to 5.5, 0 on the logo | voice, music and dense UI sound effects; the music drops out just before the logo |
| An early cut of our own launch film (2026-09) | 6.2 to 8.0 | mostly the house bed's hi-hats, few designed effects; the logo not in silence. It read busy and generic. |

Onset density counts music transients too, so it cannot tell designed effects from hi-hats. Read it together with the cue sheet:

- Action sections (demo, builds, outcomes): 2 to 3.5 designed effects per second in the cue sheet.
- Statements and held reads: at most 1 per second; let the voice and music carry.
- Logo: the sting alone, the music dropped out or faded.
